## Voice

Russian voice lines in `public/voice/` were synthesised locally with the Windows SAPI voice "Microsoft Irina" — no third-party recordings.

## Sound effects

All SFX (hit ticks, miss "glass", metronome, UI blips) are synthesised at runtime with the Web Audio API. No audio samples are shipped.
